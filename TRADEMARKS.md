# Protoboard trademark policy

> This policy may still change after legal review.

Protoboard Stack is open source. The code is licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you may use it, study it, change it, fork it and
distribute it, commercially or not, under that licence's terms.

The **name** is different. The licence covers the code, not the brand
(Apache-2.0, section 6: "This License does not grant permission to use the
trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor").
This page says how you may use the Protoboard name and logo. It follows the
approach of other open-source projects' trademark policies (the Apache
Software Foundation, Rust and Mozilla), and its purpose is the same as theirs:
when people see the name "Protoboard", they should be able to trust that it is
the real thing from Delta Robotics, Inc.

## The marks

These are trademarks of **Delta Robotics, Inc.** ("Delta"), whether or not
registered:

- the name **Protoboard** (in any capitalisation, and in names such as
  "Protoboard Stack", "Protoboard CLI" and "Protoboard Cloud");
- the **Protoboard logo**;
- the **"0" mark**, the Protoboard symbol used as the extension and app icon.

The name **UHD (Universal Hardware Description)** is covered by the UHD
project's own policy, when it has one.

## What Delta keeps

These are Delta's, and only Delta operates or publishes them:

- the **official builds** of Protoboard Stack, and the downloads site;
- the **Protoboard extension listings** on the Visual Studio Marketplace and
  Open VSX (publisher `deltarobotics`);
- the npm scope **`@protoboardai`** and the packages `@deltarobotics/*`;
- the **hosted Protoboard cloud**: the parts library, its services and
  accounts. The cloud is a separate service with its own terms, not part of
  the open-source code.

## Use that is always fine (no permission needed)

- **Using the software.** Run, change and redistribute the code under
  Apache-2.0. Keeping the copyright notices, `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, as
  the licence requires, is not a trademark use.
- **Saying what is true about compatibility (nominative use).** "Works with
  Protoboard", "a Protoboard extension", "compatible with Protoboard projects",
  "a plugin for Protoboard Stack", "based on Protoboard Stack". Use the plain
  word, not the logo, and do not make it the most prominent part of your own
  name.
- **Talking about Protoboard.** Articles, reviews, tutorials, videos, courses,
  books, talks and comparisons may use the name to refer to the project.
- **Unchanged redistribution.** Mirroring or repackaging an unmodified official
  release (for example in a Linux distribution or a company's internal
  registry) under the Protoboard name, as long as it is unmodified, or changed
  only as needed to package it for that platform.
- **Community and teaching.** User groups, meetups and courses about
  Protoboard may use the name in a descriptive way ("Boston Protoboard users"),
  as long as they do not suggest Delta runs or endorses them.

## Use that needs permission

- **Forks and modified distributions must rebrand.** Forks are welcome. If you
  distribute a modified version, give it its own name and icon: do not call it
  "Protoboard", or a name that could be confused with it ("Protoboard Plus",
  "ProtoBored", "OpenProtoboard"), and do not use the logo or the "0" mark
  as its icon. Before distributing, remove or replace the name and marks
  where they identify the product (the extension's display name and publisher,
  the command names a user sees as the product's name, the icons, the
  downloads and sign-in pages). You may keep saying truthfully where it came
  from: "a fork of Protoboard Stack".
- **No suggestion of endorsement.** Do not suggest that your product, service,
  company or event is official, endorsed, certified, sponsored or supported by
  Delta, unless it is and Delta has said so in writing.
- **Names of products, companies and domains.** Do not use "Protoboard" in the
  name of your product, company, organisation, npm scope, extension publisher,
  or in a domain name.
- **Hosted services.** Do not offer a hosted service under the Protoboard name,
  or one that presents itself as the Protoboard cloud.
- **Merchandise.** Do not sell goods carrying the name or logo.
- **Altered marks.** Do not change the logo or combine it with your own marks.

## Asking for permission

Write to **trademarks@protoboard.ai**
with what you would like to do. When in doubt, ask: a short email usually gets a
quick answer.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the name or logo used in a way that could confuse people, write to
the same address.

## Changes to this policy

Delta may update this policy. The current version is the one in the main branch
of this repository.

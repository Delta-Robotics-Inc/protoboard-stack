## Use the Protoboard library (optional)

An account is needed for the Protoboard library: its parts, their files and
prices, and publishing. Your project, the viewer, checks, builds and releases
need none.

- **Protoboard: Sign In** opens the sign-in page in your browser.
- **Protoboard: Sign In with a Code**, for a remote window (SSH, WSL, a
  container) or a machine without a browser: approve the code on any device.

Signing in sends nothing about your projects.

## Use the Protoboard library (optional)

An account is needed for the Protoboard library: its parts, their files and
prices, and publishing. Your project, the viewer, checks, builds and releases
need none.

**Protoboard: Sign In** opens the sign-in page in your browser and a prompt
at the top of this window. The prompt closes by itself when you have signed
in. In a remote window (SSH, WSL, a container), or when the browser cannot
reach this window, the page shows a code: paste it into the prompt.

Signing in sends nothing about your projects.

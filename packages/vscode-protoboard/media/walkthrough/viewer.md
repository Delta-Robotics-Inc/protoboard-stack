## See it

- **The viewer**: an editor tab with the system as a diagram, in 3D, and in
  the views a module supports (schematic, PCB, harness routes). Double-click a
  module to open it in its own tab; the **Hierarchy** button shows the tree.
- **The Protoboard Inspector** (right side bar): what the focused tab has
  selected.
- **DRC** (beside Problems): the design rule findings of the last check.
- **Protoboard Library**, **Project Parts** and **Cloud**: in the activity
  bar.

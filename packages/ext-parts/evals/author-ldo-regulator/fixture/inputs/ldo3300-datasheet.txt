1 Features
- Input voltage range: 2.5 V to 6.0 V
- Fixed output voltages: 1.8 V, 2.5 V, 3.3 V
- Output current: 300 mA
- Dropout voltage: 180 mV typical at 300 mA (3.3 V version)
- Output accuracy: ±1.5 % at 25 °C
- Quiescent current: 45 µA typical
- Active-high enable with internal 2 MΩ pull-down
- Stable with a 1 µF ceramic output capacitor
- Thermal shutdown and current limit
- Junction temperature range: -40 °C to +125 °C
- Package: SOT-23-5

3 Pin configuration and functions (SOT-23-5, top view)

  Pin   Name   I/O   Description
  1     VIN    I     Input supply. Bypass with 1 µF to GND.
  2     GND    —     Ground.
  3     EN     I     Enable. High = on (> 1.2 V), low = off (< 0.4 V).
                     Internal 2 MΩ pull-down. Do not leave floating in noisy
                     environments.
  4     NC     —     No internal connection. Leave open or connect to GND.
  5     VOUT   O     Regulated output. Bypass with ≥ 1 µF ceramic to GND.

4 Absolute maximum ratings
  VIN, EN to GND ................................. -0.3 V to 6.5 V
  VOUT to GND .................................... -0.3 V to VIN + 0.3 V
  Junction temperature ........................... +150 °C
  Storage temperature ............................ -65 °C to +150 °C

5 Recommended operating conditions
  VIN ............................................ 2.5 V to 6.0 V
  EN ............................................. 0 V to VIN
  Output current ................................. 0 mA to 300 mA
  Junction temperature ........................... -40 °C to +125 °C

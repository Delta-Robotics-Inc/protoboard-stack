# Harnesses and CAD

How the watch's physical parts fit together, where each one's geometry comes
from, and how to regenerate it. Decisions are numbered as in
[decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Stack-up

**Case frame.** +Z points out of the back cover, towards the wrist. z = 0 is
the display's viewing face, on the lens. The board frame is the case frame
seen from the back cover: the board's top copper faces the back cover.

The parts, front (z = 0) to back:

| Part | z (mm) | Held by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMMA lens, 38 × 0.5 mm | −0.5 to 0, in a pocket of the 1.3 mm floor | the pocket (the window's cover) |
| Display ER-TFT1.28-1 | 0 to about 1.5 | the board, through its FPC (`ds1`, bottom side); bonded to the floor (D-W2, D-W6) |
| Cell LP402030, 4.0 mm | 1.75 to 5.75, on 0.25 mm foam tape; 0.53 mm clear of the board to swell (D-W15) | foam tape; placement in `.protoboard/display.json` |
| Main board, 0.8 mm | mid-plane 6.68; parts on its top face up to 10.39 (USB-C) | the walls and the cavity; placement in `.protoboard/display.json` |
| LRA VG0832012 | can on the back cover's inner face (10.64) | adhesive pad (D-W6) |
| Back cover (lid), 1.3 mm | 10.64 to 11.94, with the heart-rate window | four M2 × 6 screws into heat-set inserts |

The display's FPC drops 4.65 mm past the cell to its land on the board's
bottom face. Its contacts are 4.78 mm below the board's bottom copper. The
cell's leads come off its PCM end (+y) to J3, also on the bottom face. The
LRA's leads reach J4 on the top face.

Overall: a round case with a 44.7 mm outer diameter, 62 mm across the lugs,
13.49 mm thick (brief: 13.5 mm).

## Geometry sources

| What | Generated by | Sources | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main board (copper, parts, display) | circuit capability, `@protoboardai/ext-tscircuit` | `watch-main.uhd.ts`, `circuit.config.ts`, `tscircuit/footprints.json`, `tscircuit/layout.json`; part STEP models from the catalog and `parts/local` | `src/boards/watch-main/out/`: Gerbers, drill, BOM, pick-and-place, netlist, `board.step`, `board.glb`, `board.manifest.json` |
| Case (base, back cover, two plungers) | cad capability, `@protoboardai/ext-enclosure-cad` (build123d) | `watch-case.uhd.ts` (features), `cad.config.ts`, the board's manifest (USB-C mouth, switch stems, heart-rate apertures), the display's viewing face | `src/enclosures/watch-case/out/`: STEP, GLB, STL and 3MF per body, manifest; `watch-case.bind.ts` binds the frames |
| Cell and LRA leads | `@protoboardai/ext-harness-cad` | the harness modules and their `cad.config.ts`; the assembled model | `src/harnesses/<id>/`: routed GLB and manifest, `routes.ts` binds them |
| Display, cell, spring bar, 20 pF capacitor | part CAD (`parts/local/cadkit`) | the part's drawing (see each part's evidence) | `parts/local/<id>/` |

## Harnesses

| Harness | Carries | Ends | Conductors | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `watch-battery-leads` | the cell's battery output and the charger's output (UHD derives both through it) | the cell's own leads ↔ J3 pads (+ to 1) | 2 × AWG30 UL1007, red and black | 7.5 mm installed (cut to 9 mm from the supplied 50 mm, 1.5 mm stripped) |
| `watch-lra-leads` | the DRV2605L's differential drive | the LRA's own leads ↔ J4 pads (+ to 1) | 2 fine leads, red and blue | 6 mm installed (cut to 7.5 mm from the supplied 10 mm) |
| `watch-back-cover-screws` | the back-cover joint | lid ↔ base | 4 × M2 × 6 pan-head A2 screws, 4 × ruthex RX-M2x4 inserts | — |

The lead lengths are the shortest clear route that harness-cad reports, plus
0.3 to 0.5 mm to strip and solder. The pads J3 and J4 are sites of the board
(`site_j3`, `site_j4`), so each lead's board end lands on a frame.

## Regenerate

From `examples/wearable-watch`, with `PROTOBOARD_PYTHON` pointing at a Python
that has build123d:

```sh
npx protoboard run circuit:generate --module watch-main-board   # board, out/
npx protoboard run firmware:generate --module watch-main-board  # zephyr/
npx protoboard run cad:generate --module watch-case             # case bodies
npx protoboard run cad:export --module watch-case               # STEP / GLB / STL / 3MF
npx protoboard run cad:generate --module watch-battery-leads    # routed leads
npx protoboard run cad:generate --module watch-lra-leads
npx protoboard check
```

After the case or the board changes, regenerate `watch-case.bind.ts` from
the case's `cad:extract` output. Its frames and artifacts are copied into the
module, the same way the quadcopter's frame is.

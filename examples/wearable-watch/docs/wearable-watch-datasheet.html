00The productWearable watch

What it is

A small, open, fully described smartwatch: a round colour display, motion and heart-rate sensing, haptic alerts and Bluetooth LE to a phone.

Features

  • A 1.28-inch round 240×240 colour display
  • Step counting, wrist-raise wake and heart rate on demand
  • Haptic alerts, and two buttons on the case side
  • Bluetooth LE to a phone
  • A 200 mAh LiPo cell charged from USB-C; target of 5 days or more of typical use
  • A printed case for a standard 22 mm two-piece strap; splash resistant, no ingress rating claimed

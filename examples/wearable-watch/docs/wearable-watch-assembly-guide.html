  1. Seat the Display lens, 38 × 0.5 mm PMMA onto the display window and lens.
  2. Seat the Heart-rate window cover, 4.0 × 8.2 × 0.5 mm PMMA onto the heart-rate window.
  3. Fit the Clear UV-curing adhesive for the window covers.

TIP Peel the protective film from the inner face only. Put a thin bead of adhesive on each pocket's ledge, press the cover in flush and cure it before anything goes inside the case.
CAUTION Keep adhesive off the apertures: on the heart-rate window it would scatter the sensor's light (OQ-W7).

3

Fold the cell and the display, and seat the board in the case

A×1
Watch main board
B×1
LiPol LP402030 200 mAh 3.7 V LiPo with PCM
C×1
Cell foam pad, 0.25 mm double-sided
D×1
Display adhesive ring, 0.25 mm
  1. Place the Watch main board as shown. It has no mechanical interface in the model; the figure places it by .protoboard/display.json: mid-plane at z 6.68: the display it carries on its rear face (soldered by the FPC, 4.78 mm below the rear copper) puts the viewing face at z 0 on the lens; its components face the back cover; the USB-C mouth and the switch stems line up with the case wall at this height (the case is generated around it).
  2. Place the LiPol LP402030 200 mAh 3.7 V LiPo with PCM as shown. It has no mechanical interface in the model; the figure places it by .protoboard/display.json: between the display and the board, PCM end towards 12 o'clock as seen from the back (+y), on 0.25 mm foam tape on the display's rear face, 0.53 mm clear of the board for the cell to swell (D-W15); held by the foam tape (D-W6).
  3. Fit the Cell foam pad, 0.25 mm double-sided.
  4. Fit the Display adhesive ring, 0.25 mm.
  5. Fold the cell under the board's rear face on its leads, PCM end towards 12 o'clock, and stick it to the display's rear face with the foam pad.
  6. Fold the display over the cell on its FPC, viewing face out.
  7. Stick the adhesive ring to the display's front rim, outside its visual area, and lower the board and display into the case: display face onto the floor, USB-C into its opening, button stems behind their plungers.
Step 3 of 7
CAUTION Do not crease the display's FPC: it bends once, past the cell's edge, as it left the board unit.

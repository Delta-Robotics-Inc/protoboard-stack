# Wearable watch: open questions

Decisions and unknowns handed to later tracks. Each has a proposed answer so a
track can proceed; the owner confirms or overrides. Source tags refer to the
[component selection sources](component-selection.md#sources).

| OQ-W1 | The ER-TFT1.28-1 has a 12-pin, 0.7 mm pitch solder-tab FPC [W3], not a ZIF tail. Solder it to pads on the board, or choose a display with a ZIF-connector FPC? | Solder to pads as a post-reflow hot-bar or hand step, documented in the assembly guide. Re-evaluate the Winstar WFN0128A2T0YADNN000 [W4] once its FPC pitch and connector are known; if it takes a standard ZIF connector, prefer it for machine assembly. | parts research, board | Board outline near the display tail |
| OQ-W2 | Supply the nRF52840 from 3V0 in normal voltage mode, or from VBAT through VDDH (high voltage mode, REG0 on)? | 3V0 normal mode for v0.1: one rail for the module and the display, as the display requires [W3] §6.2. Raytac recommends VDDH supply when the input can exceed 3.6 V [W1] §8.1–8.2; REG0 can then power external circuits, but only up to 25 mA [W2], which the display (10.5 mA max) plus sensors fit. Revisit if the 3.0 V LDO's loss matters (about 20 % at 3.8 V input). | board | Rail plan |
| OQ-W3 | Does the charger need a power path (system load separate from the charging current), and does charging need temperature qualification? | Not for v0.1: MCP73831 with the system on VBAT. Known limits: termination can be missed while the watch is active [W10]; no thermistor input and no NTC in the cell [W10, W12]. The leading alternative is TI BQ25120A (I2C charger, 25-ball DSBGA [W21]; **Assumption:** integrated regulators and power path, datasheet not yet read). Decide before board layout. | board | Charger choice |
| OQ-W4 | USB-C receptacle or pogo pins? | USB-C (USB4105-GF-A [W13]) for v0.1: no dock to design, and USB data allows firmware updates over USB. Pogo pins would allow a sealed case but need a dock or a magnetic cable; reconsider if a water rating becomes a goal. | enclosure, board | Case opening |
| OQ-W5 | Stack order inside the case. The heart-rate sensor must face the skin; the display faces up; the cell (4.0 mm [W12]) and the board both fit under a 35.6 × 37.8 mm display [W3]. | Display / cell / board, with U3 on the board's skin side behind a window in the back cover. The display FPC folds past the cell to the board; check that its length (about 12 mm from the drawing [W3], **Assumption:** reading of the drawing) reaches. Fallback: display / board / cell with U3 on a small satellite board and a flex harness. | enclosure, board | Board outline, case height |
| OQ-W6 | Gate the display's VDD with a load switch to remove its 15 µA sleep current [W3]? | Not for v0.1; it is about 25 % of the sleep floor but about 3 % of a typical day's charge (0.36 of 14.03 mAh) (see [power budget](power-budget.md)). Note that the display's datasheet requires the display VDD to power everything that drives its bus [W3] §6.2, so gating would also need the nRF52840's SPI pins held low. | board, firmware | — |

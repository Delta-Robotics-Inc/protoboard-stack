00The productSmart water bottle

What it is

A cap module that replaces the stock lid of a 32 oz Nalgene wide-mouth bottle and reports how much water is left and how warm it is, reminds the user to drink with a light ring, and syncs readings over Wi-Fi.

Features

  • Water level and temperature measured from the cap, with nothing in the bottle but a sealed stainless probe
  • A ring of 8 RGB LEDs for reminders and status, and one button
  • Wi-Fi sync, with provisioning over Bluetooth LE
  • A 1000 mAh LiPo cell charged from USB-C through an IP67 receptacle; target of 30 days or more between charges
  • A printed PETG cap that seals on the bottle's lip; hand wash only, no ingress rating claimed

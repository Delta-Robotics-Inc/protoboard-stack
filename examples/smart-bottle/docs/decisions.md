# Smart water bottle: decisions

Design decisions taken while building the project, with the
requirement or finding behind each. Open questions from the brief keep their
ids (OQ-B*, [open questions](open-questions.md)); decisions here are D-B*.
Source tags refer to the [component selection sources](component-selection.md#sources).

## Decisions

| Id | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-B01 | Catalog parts are vendored from the read-only dev catalog (`cloud.url` http://localhost:8795); the bottle, the cell and the probe are project-researched (`parts/local`), copied from the parts workspace (commit 9a80317) and re-verified with `part-verify`. | Brief part tiers; the 22 bottle parts other than these three are in the catalog. |
| D-B02 | `nalgene-32oz-wide-mouth` and `adafruit-381-ds18b20-probe` categories moved to the UHD taxonomy 1.0.0 (`mechanical.enclosure`, `sensor.environmental`). | `part-verify` rejected the pre-taxonomy categories; recorded as repairs in their verification records. |
| D-B03 | The probe's and the cell's CAD now draw only their rigid parts: the stainless tube plus 6 mm of heat shrink, and 2 mm lead stubs. The cable and the leads are the harnesses `bottle-probe-cable` and `bottle-battery-leads`. | Drawn straight, the probe's sleeves, jacket and wires stood 134 mm above the tube and the cell's leads 50 mm out of the cap; both bend, so they are routed cables. The CAD generators run in the project (`parts/local/_cadkit`). |
| D-B04 | TPS7A02 is not used. | The bottle's selection makes 3V3 with the TPS63020 buck-boost over the whole cell range; the TPS7A02 belongs to the watch. |
| D-B05 | Board: Ø70.0 mm, four layers, 0.6 mm FR-4. | The USB4720 mid-mount needs a 0.6 ± 0.1 mm board (GCT); the cavity is Ø71.4 mm, and Ø70.4 left only 0.12 mm to the wall in the cap's fit check. Two layers routed in 18-24 min with 60-150 DRC errors around the module; four layers route in about 3 min, clean (D-B15). |
| D-B06 | Charger STAT reaches GPIO7 through 68 kΩ / 100 kΩ (47 kΩ in the first layout). | STAT drives high to VDD (up to 5.5 V on USB); the ESP32-C3's inputs take at most VDD + 0.3 V. 68k/100k gives 3.27 V at 5.5 V and 2.83 V at 4.75 V, above VIH = 0.75 × 3.28 V = 2.46 V [B1]; 47k gave 3.74 V at 5.5 V. The "no battery" (high-Z) state reads low (change from docs/system.md). The firmware config now names the line (`BOARD_U5_STAT_GPIO`): core's firmware map follows a series resistor to the part it reaches. |
| D-B07 | Every land pattern is the maker's recommended one, verified against its drawing: ext-tscircuit's table holds the purchased parts' (GCT USB4720 rev B with its four plated stake slots, two plated tag holes and paired contacts on one land; Espressif Figure 11-1 with the 3 × 3 ground pads; Alps SKRT Drawing No.1 with the Ø0.9 mm guide-boss holes; TI 4219108/B and 4210895-2/E with their thermal-pad stencils; Microchip C04-2123B; Maxim 90-0065; ST DS4260 Figure 19 and DS13812 Figure 19; Coilcraft 745-3; Kingbright DSAO4453; Yageo and Murata reflow lands), each with a verification record; `tscircuit/footprints.json` holds only the board-copper pads, each citing the pad part. `circuit:check` shows no `CIRCUIT_FOOTPRINT_UNVERIFIED`, so the board's work order has no hold. | The first revision's patterns were derived from the parts' CAD (`scripts/derive-footprints.mjs`, removed) and unverified, with surface pads standing in for the receptacle's slots and the switch's holes: no mechanical retention for the IP67 receptacle. The receptacle's tail lands moved 0.05 mm (to GCT's tail line), its pair lands became one 0.65 mm land each, and its slots and holes are drilled. The ST VL53L4CD has no separate land in its data sheet; its lands are its pads 1:1 (recorded). |
| D-B08 | The cap is one module (base and lid printed parts) generated by ext-enclosure-cad from its features; the lid is a bayonet sealed by an AS568-040 radial O-ring on the base's collar (D-B09). | Brief: a sealed lid. The first revision closed a flat joint with six M2 screws into heat-set inserts (component selection, fasteners), which put the cap past Ø80 (D-B09). |
| D-B09 | Envelope requirement (Ø ≤ 80 mm) **met**: the lid is a bayonet (ext-enclosure-cad `TwistLid`, three lugs, 30° turn), so the six screw lugs (they reached Ø91.6 mm) are gone and the cap is Ø80.0 mm overall. The base's wall steps in to a Ø76 mm collar from z 12.3 to the cavity top; the lid's skirt (Ø80 outside) slides over it, its L-slots take the lugs, and an AS568-040 in a groove round the collar seals radially against the skirt's bore (Parker Design Chart 4-1: 1.30 mm gland depth, 27 % squeeze ±2.5 % with the collar off centre, 1.0 % stretch). The lug on +Y is a 10 mm key and the other two are 6 mm: the key enters only its own slot, so the lid goes on in one position (the report's `lid_positions` 1; with three equal lugs it went on in three, 120° apart, and in two of them the light pipes stood 15° off their LEDs, since the LEDs are 45° apart). The skirt's bore is drawn Ø76.29, 0.1 mm (radial) over the Ø76.09 it should print at (`boreAllowanceMm`): a printed bore comes out undersize, and printed at Ø76.09 it gives Parker's 0.089 mm diametral fit on the Ø76.0 collar, with the groove's depth measured from it. If it prints at its drawn size, the squeeze falls to 13.5–29.7 % with the collar off centre (22–32 % wanted) (the check's info line), so the printed bore is measured first (OQ-B16). The cavity top went from 16.8 to 20.6 mm so that the collar sits above the USB-C counterbore and the button's bore (the lid turns: nothing may cut both); the cap stands 23.1 mm above the lip (brief ≤ 45). The board stays Ø70. The `design_envelope` is the brief's Ø80. The six M2 lid screws and six inserts are gone (the cap holds four inserts, for the board). | The 0.1.0 cap's lid seal was a face seal in the base rim, which pushed the screw columns outside it, past Ø80 and wider than the Ø89 bottle. A radial seal on a collar needs no rim, and a twist lid needs no columns. Bayonet rather than thread: with the key the lid goes on one way, and the slot ends fix its angle, so the eight light pipes stand over the LEDs; a thread's end position varies with the tolerances (about ±18° per 0.1 mm at a 2 mm pitch). Printed fit (0.2 mm radial and 0.15 mm axial clearance at the lugs; the seal bore's 0.1 mm allowance over Parker's 0.089 mm fit) is an assumption to check on the first print (OQ-B16). |
| D-B10 | The USB-C port is a stepped opening: GCT's 8.44 × 2.66 mm plug aperture, and a 12.75 × 6.9 × 3.0 mm counterbore outside for the plug's overmold. The receptacle sits 1.5 mm inside the board edge (its tail lands are kept clear by the `usb_tail_edge` keep-out). It is modelled with the provider's window feature (aperture plus outside pocket, nothing fitted). | GCT's panel is 1.8 mm and a mated plug's overmold stands 2.2 mm in front of it, so the receptacle may sit at most 4.0 mm behind the outer face; the wall is 4.3 mm and curved. With the receptacle at the board edge its tongue struck the wall in the fit check; 1.5 mm inside and a 3.0 mm counterbore clear it. The provider has no stepped opening (stack feedback). |
| D-B11 | Fault scenarios: six system faults (`src/scenarios.ts`) and five firmware faults (`scripts/firmware-scenarios.ts`). | UHD cannot see the I2C bus on this board (the ESP32-C3's I2C is in its GPIO matrix; the part states no I2C controller), so addresses, pins and wake sources are checked by the firmware rules, and the release's bring-up reads the bus from the board's firmware map (bus scan at 0x28, 0x29, 0x36 and a smoke test per device). |
| D-B12 | Light pipes: Ø3 mm holes over the LEDs, filled with clear UV-cure resin after printing. | OQ-B7; the provider prints base and lid in one material. |
| D-B13 | Wake from Deep-sleep: the button only (GPIO3); samples on the RTC timer. | The ToF is in hardware standby while asleep; the fuel gauge's alert is on GPIO6, which cannot wake the chip [B2]. |
| D-B14 | No via in any SMD pad (ext-tscircuit `via_in_pad: "avoid"`, the default; `circuit:check` reports any as `CIRCUIT_VIA_IN_PAD`), so nothing needs filling and capping. Power traces 0.25 mm. | The first layout let the router put vias in pads and asked the fab to fill them. With vias kept out, the router left one via on D9's pad; D9 moved 0.2 mm (+X) and the board routed clean. A 0.25 mm trace carries the 303 mA charge and the 350 mA Wi-Fi peaks with margin on 1 oz copper. |
| D-B15 | Placement by hand (`tscircuit/layout.json`), routing by the provider's autorouter; a keep-out at the USB tail edge and the antenna keep-out at the rim. Positions that left the router stuck (the 3V3 test pad, a via at a module pad corner) were moved by small rerolls. | The autorouter places nothing; `sequential_trace` routed nothing on this board. |
| D-B16 | The cap's probe passes through the floor at (-20.5, 0) and the cavity top is 16.8 mm, so the probe's heat shrink and the routed cable clear the lid. Cable lengths are the routed lengths: 9 mm for the cell leads, 16 mm for the probe cable. | The routed cable hit the lid at a 16.0 mm cavity; a length longer than the route makes the router add a slack loop that does not fit the cap. |
| D-B17 | VBUS is sensed on GPIO10 through 68 kΩ / 100 kΩ (100k/100k in the first layout). | GPIO10 is a digital input on the C3 (no ADC). 100k/100k gave 2.38 V at the USB minimum of 4.75 V, below VIH (2.46 V at the 3.28 V rail): undefined. 68k/100k gives 2.83 V at 4.75 V and 3.27 V at 5.5 V. The firmware config names it (`BOARD_VBUS_SENSE_GPIO`, pulled down by r18). |
| D-B18 | The VL53L4CD sits 3 mm from the cap's axis (it was 18.4 mm off it), its decoupling (c4, c5) above it on the top side; the cap's ToF window follows it. | With an 18° field of view (±9°), the cone from 18.4 mm off axis met the bottle's side wall well above the 210 mm floor, so wall returns would bias readings near empty. From 3 mm off axis it reaches the floor within Ø73 mm, inside the Ø89 bottle. The board was re-routed (about 4 min, 0 errors) and the cap regenerated. OQ-B1 (water surface, window) stays open. |
| D-B19 | Units: the cap unit is the printed housing with its four heat-set inserts set (they are its children) and the light pipes filled; it ends there. The button plunger, the three O-rings, the board, the cell and the probe are fitted at final assembly (system/), in that order, and the bayonet lid closes it (no lid screws since D-B09). | The first package's cap work order fitted the O-rings and the plunger and closed the lid before final assembly, which then had to open it again and repeated the inserts and O-rings. The enclosure provider's print order now stops at the inserts and names what assembly fits; the seal, button and lid-joint checks move to the system test plan. |
| D-B20 | The board states an estimated 9 g (`bottle-cap-board` mechanical weight_g, assumption trait). | Its 61 parts state no mass, so the cap weight left the board out. Estimate: FR-4 and copper about 5.5 g, the module about 1.5 g, the receptacle about 0.8 g, the rest about 1 g. Weigh the first board. |
| D-B21 | The outline follows GCT's recommended layout for the USB4720: the cut-out's rear edge 0.55 mm in front of the tail line (y = 28.30, was 28.5) and the board edge 1.28 mm in front of the front stake slots (y = 33.33 over ±5 mm, was 32.7). JPRB moved 0.1 mm (+X) and D9 0.2 mm (+X). | At y = 32.7 the front stake slots (to y = 33.125) would have broken out of the edge. With IPC-7351B courtyards the probe pads touched LED 1's; D9: D-B14. The board was re-routed (4 layers, 0 errors) and re-exported; the cap and the probe cable were regenerated from it. |

## Warnings dispositioned

Every warning `protoboard check` reports, and why it stays:

| Code | Where | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `FW_STRAPPING_PIN` (2) | `bottle-cap-board` u1 GPIO2 (SCL), GPIO8 (SDA) | Accepted (OQ-B12): the 4.7 kΩ pull-ups hold both high at reset; SPI boot ignores GPIO8, download boot needs it high, GPIO2 should be high [B1] Table 4-3. |

Info notes (not warnings): `HW_NOT_VERIFIED` for catalog parts with no
local verification record. `UHD_UNPLACED` for the board's 61 components,
which are drawn in the board's own body, and for the cap's four inserts,
which are drawn in neither (they sit inside the bosses). GPIO7 and GPIO10
are no longer reported as unused (`FW_NET_UNUSED`): the firmware map follows
their resistors (D-B06, D-B17).

The viewer screenshots in `docs/images/` were taken with a uhd-viewer built
against this branch's UHD (uhd 53a6166, `atRail` and the composite-pair
preference), so they show no findings that `check` does not. A viewer built
against an older UHD shows two stale `LINK STATE` findings, on the charger
and the ToF supply.
